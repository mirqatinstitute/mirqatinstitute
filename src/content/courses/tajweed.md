## What is Tajweed?

Tajweed literally means *to improve* or *to make excellent*. In recitation, it is the set of rules for pronouncing every letter of the Qur'an correctly — giving each letter its proper point of articulation and characteristics, and observing the rules for lengthening, merging, nasalising and stopping.

Reciting with Tajweed preserves the meaning of the words and the beauty of the recitation as it has been passed down from teacher to student.

## How our Tajweed classes work

Tajweed is learned by listening and being corrected, which is why every Mirqat class is **one-to-one and online**. Your teacher explains a rule, demonstrates it, and then listens to your recitation — correcting your pronunciation until the rule becomes second nature.

Our teaching is grounded in **Hafs and Shu'bah Riwayah certifications**, and classes are scheduled in **IST (GMT+5:30)** at times that suit you.
