## About Qur'an Reading

Knowing the rules of Tajweed is one step — reading smoothly and accurately, page after page, is the next. This course is regular, guided recitation practice that turns knowledge into fluency.

## How classes work

Classes are **one-to-one and online**. You read; your teacher listens, corrects your pronunciation and helps you build a steady, confident pace. Timings are flexible and scheduled in **IST (GMT+5:30)**.
