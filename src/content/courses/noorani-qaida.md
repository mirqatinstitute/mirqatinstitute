## What is Noorani Qaida?

Noorani Qaida is a beginner's primer for learning to read the Qur'an. It starts with the individual Arabic letters and builds, lesson by lesson, to complete words and sentences — so that by the end, a student can open the Mushaf and read.

It is the first step on the Mirqat path. Getting these foundations right — the correct sound of every letter, and how vowels change it — makes everything that follows, from Tajweed to Hifz, much easier.

## How our Noorani Qaida classes work

Every class is **one-to-one and online**, so the teacher can listen closely to each sound the student makes and correct it straight away. Young children get short, encouraging lessons; adults can move faster through the parts they find easy and spend longer where they need it.

Classes are scheduled in **IST (GMT+5:30)** at times that suit the student, and families anywhere in the world can join.
