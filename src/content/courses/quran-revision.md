## Why revision matters

Memorising the Qur'an is only half the journey — keeping it is the other half. Without regular, structured revision (muraja'ah), even well-memorised portions fade. This programme gives Hifz students a clear system and a teacher to recite to.

## How revision classes work

Classes are **one-to-one and online**. You recite from memory, your teacher listens and corrects, and together you work through a customised revision schedule that keeps every portion strong. Timings are flexible and scheduled in **IST (GMT+5:30)**.
