## Memorising the Qur'an, step by step

Hifz is one of the most rewarding journeys a student can take — and one that needs structure, consistency and a teacher who checks every line. Our Hifz programme gives each student a **personalised plan**, then works through it with new lessons, daily revision and regular assessment.

## How our Hifz classes work

Every class is **one-to-one and online**. Your teacher listens to your new lesson (sabak), checks your revision (muraja'ah), corrects your Tajweed and pronunciation, and tracks your progress over time.

Choose **20, 25 or 30 classes a month** depending on how quickly you want to progress. Timings are flexible and scheduled in **IST (GMT+5:30)**.
