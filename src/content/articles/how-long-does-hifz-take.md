## A simple way to estimate

The commonly used Mushaf has **604 pages**. If you know how much a student will memorise on each lesson day, you can estimate the time for the new memorisation:

| New memorisation | Every day | 5 days a week |
|---|---|---|
| 2 pages a day | about 10 months | about 14 months |
| 1 page a day | about 20 months | about 2 years 4 months |
| ½ page a day | about 3 years 4 months | about 4 years 8 months |
| ¼ page a day | about 6 years 7 months | about 9 years 3 months |

These figures cover **new lessons only**. In practice, extra time is needed for revision, tests, holidays and slower periods — so a realistic plan adds a margin on top.

## What affects the timeline?

- **Consistency.** Daily, steady progress matters far more than occasional long sessions.
- **Revision.** The faster a student memorises, the more they must revise. Revision is what makes memorisation last, so it should never be skipped to "save time".
- **Reading and Tajweed.** Students who already read fluently with Tajweed memorise faster and make fewer mistakes that need correcting later.
- **Age and routine.** Children and adults can both complete Hifz; what matters most is a routine that fits the student's life.
- **Teacher guidance.** A teacher who hears each lesson, corrects mistakes immediately and adjusts the daily amount helps the student keep a pace they can sustain.

## Full-time or part-time?

Students who study full-time can memorise more each day and usually finish sooner. Students who memorise alongside school or work usually take longer — and that is completely fine. A steady part-time pace that the student can keep up for years is better than a fast pace that leads to burnout or forgetting.

## How revision fits in

Most Hifz programmes split each day into a new lesson (*sabak*), recent revision (*sabqi*) and older revision (*manzil*). As the amount memorised grows, more time goes to revision — which is why progress can feel slower in later years. This is normal and is what keeps the whole Qur'an strong.

## The most important thing

Hifz is not a race. A personalised plan, daily practice and regular revision with a teacher are what bring a student to completion — and keep the Qur'an with them for life.
