FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp or email us.

Who can join?

Children, youth and adults of all learning levels. Every class is one-to-one, so lessons are matched to each student.

Do I need any prior Qur'an knowledge?

No. Complete beginners start with Noorani Qaida and basic Qur'an reading.

Do you offer Hifz?

Yes. Our Hifz plan includes a personalised plan, new lessons (sabak), daily muraja'ah and progress tracking, plus a separate Qur'an revision programme for Hifz students.

What are the class timings?

Timings are flexible. Classes are scheduled in IST (GMT+5:30), with slots arranged around the student's availability.

How much do classes cost?

Plans start from ₹1,000 per month for Hifz and ₹1,300 per month for the Basic Qur'an and Tajweed plans. See pricing for all options.

How do I enrol?

Contact us on WhatsApp or through the enrolment form, choose a course and class timing, complete your registration, and begin classes.
