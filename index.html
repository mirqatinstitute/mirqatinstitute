The Mirqat path

Every student has a next step.

Wherever you are starting from, we assess your level and place you on the right rung — then climb with you, steadily, until the Qur'an lives in your heart and on your tongue.

  1. 01

    Noorani Qaida

    Arabic letters, sounds and joining — the foundation for reading correctly.

  2. 02

    Nazirah

    Fluent reading of the Mushaf from sight, building confidence page by page.

  3. 03

    Tajweed

    The rules of beautiful recitation — makharij, sifaat, madd and more.

  4. 04

    Hifz

    Structured memorisation with daily sabaq, sabqi and manzil revision.

  5. 05

    Ijazah

    Complete recitation to a certified sheikh and join an unbroken chain to the Prophet ﷺ.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Email us and we'll reply within a day.

Is the trial class really free?

Yes. The first session is completely free with no obligation. Your teacher will assess your level and recommend the right course and plan.

What do I need to join a class?

A laptop, tablet or phone with a stable internet connection and a headset if possible. We'll send you a link before each class.

What age can my child start?

Most children are ready from age 5. For younger learners, sessions are shorter and more playful to keep them engaged.

Do you have female teachers?

Yes. Sisters and young girls can request a female teacher for any course.

What happens if we miss a class?

Let us know at least 12 hours in advance and we'll reschedule at no extra cost.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month to month. Just let us know before your next billing date.
